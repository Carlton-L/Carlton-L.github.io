How the operators change pages

The background is settled: the dither field persists and a heat band crosses it. This page compares what the operators do on top of that. Pick a direction, then click around the nav. SLOW ×4 helps you see the order of things.

1 · WIPE IN THE REPO NOW

The new page is revealed from the top behind a soft edge, in step with the band. The old page stays still underneath. Operators are cut by the edge as it passes.

One image of each page, so it costs almost nothing.

2 · HANDOFF + WIPE

The same wipe, and each new operator also eases into place from a few pixels off as the edge reaches it. The network looks like it receives the signal and settles.

Two motions on the same operators at once. Check whether it reads as richer or as busy.

3 · HANDOFF RECOMMENDED

No wipe edge. Old operators switch off one by one as the band passes them, and new ones ease in behind it. Only the background and the operators move.

Needs a little more work in the real site: old operators have to leave before the swap, or each needs its own transition name.

4 · PAN

The old network slides out and the new one slides in, as if the camera moved across one big patch. Going deeper pans right, going back pans left. The band rides the seam between the two pages, so it runs sideways here.

It moves the whole screen, which is the most motion of the four. In READ mode it moves a column of text sideways.

NOTES

DIM OLD PAGE fades the outgoing page to 35% during directions 1 and 2, so the eye goes to what is arriving.

REDUCED MOTION turns every direction into a plain 300ms fade with no band.
