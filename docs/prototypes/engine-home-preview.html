Decision

Carlton picked A and R1 on 2026-11-14. A reads at viewer size, costs 2 KB and nothing on selection, and fits the 7.2 s cycle; R1 keeps the homepage at about zero framework JS. Built in src/lib/previs.js (etDraw); the rule is in docs/PATCH_SYSTEM.md, "Shared previews".

The question

PR #45 put the real Engine demo in the case study's hero, playing a scripted tour with seven captions, each tagged with its trust-triangle corner. The homepage still shows the old preview (a growing knowledge graph with five phase ticks), which no longer looks like the page it opens. How should the preview echo the hero, and what may a heavy preview load, and when?

Fixed: the viewer is 420 × 240 (SVG viewBox), about 560 px wide at 1440 and 343 px at 375. The home cycle moves to the next row every 7.2 s, so a preview gets 7.2 s unless the visitor holds it. The Engine is the sixth of seven rows, reached about 36 s after load by the cycle.

Directions

A · Drawn tour RECOMMENDED

A new V entry in previs.js, drawn the way every other preview is. Three of the hero's moments in one 7.2 s cycle, one per corner, with the hero's own captions and tag: the crash on Groundwork (OBSERVE), the pointer presses Retry and the research files (STEER), then a cut to the report where the $4.1B shows its source (TRACE). Same dark ground, banners, pointer, caption card and green signal as the hero; the type is large enough to read at 343 px. Reduced motion shows the TRACE frame still. INVERT swaps to the paper palette.

B · Recorded stills

Three frames captured from the hero itself (captions burned in), cross-fading with a slow push-in, fetched on first selection. Exactly the hero's pixels, but at viewer width the product text is 5 to 8 px, the stills go stale with every demo change unless re-recorded, and a still cannot show the crash turning into a retry. A video would show motion but costs far more (estimate below) and still needs re-recording.

C · The real demo, loaded on selection

The gl pattern from grid_lamp, with an iframe: when the row is selected, load /demos/engine/ into the viewer and run a short script. The truest echo, and its download is lighter than grid_lamp's. But the demo is laid out for 1100 × 780: shrunk into the viewer it is unreadable (the still here is the demo at its own size, scaled to fit). The hero's 40 s tour does not fit 7.2 s, so it needs a second script to maintain. It still needs a drawn fallback for the load gap, reduced motion and failure. And the cycle would load it for every visitor who stays 36 s.

Why A

  • It is the only option where the words are readable at the viewer's size, and the words (the captions, the corners) are what tie it to the hero.
  • It keeps the homepage at about zero framework JS, and selecting the row costs nothing.
  • It fits the 7.2 s cycle exactly, and loops cleanly when a visitor holds the row.
  • It is honest in the way the other minis are: a drawing labelled RECREATION, opening a page where the real thing plays.

Choosing A means not showing STEER's rerun or the prompt parts on the homepage. If Carlton wants those, A can take a fourth beat by stretching to two cycles; the cycle would move on halfway through.

A rule for heavy previews

Every case study is moving to a real demo in its hero. This decides what the homepage and /projects viewer may load for one, and when.

R1 · Previews are drawings RECOMMENDED

  • First load: the viewer's whole cost is previs.js. Budget: previs.js at most 24 KB gz (19.7 with A), each entry at most 3 KB gz.
  • On selection: nothing. Real product code lives on the case-study page, which already reserves its space and loads after the page change.
  • Echo the hero: a preview borrows the hero's moments, captions and palette, compressed to one 7.2 s cycle, and is labelled RECREATION when the hero is a demo.
  • grid_lamp stays the one exception, as now. A new exception needs Carlton's call and must meet R2's terms.

R2 · Drawings first, upgrade on intent

  • Every preview has a drawing. A heavy upgrade (3D, a real demo) loads only on a deliberate selection: hover held 600 ms, focus, or a tap. Never from the cycle clock.
  • Budget for an upgrade: 250 KB gz, nothing on the main thread over 50 ms, paused off screen, the drawing underneath until it is ready, the drawing only under reduced motion.
  • This would change grid_lamp, which today loads on a cycle tick.

R3 · Recorded media for heavy previews

  • A preview whose real form is heavy ships a recorded poster (at most 60 KB) fetched on first selection, with a drawing as the poster's placeholder.
  • Cheap to make once, but stale after each demo change, and text at viewer width is unreadable.

Recommendation: R1. It keeps the homepage's promise (about zero framework JS, nothing heavy on a cycle tick from new previews) and the reading problem settles it: at 420 × 240, a real product UI is a texture, while a drawing can choose what to say. R2 is the right rule if Carlton wants grid_lamp's 3D kept and more like it.

Limits of this prototype

  • A is the code that would go into previs.js, running at 30 fps as the engine does. The dither dissolve between scenes is left out.
  • B's stills are real captures of the hero from the local build. C is simulated with a still of the demo at its own size; live it would play. The load time is an experiment control.
  • The index is static. The rack is a sketch of the home layout, not the field.
