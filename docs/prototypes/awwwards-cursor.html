What the pointer shows you

A mock of the home network. The pointer already paints heat into the background, as on the site. Two additions sit on top: the signal trace and a typed verb beside the pointer. Hover the operators and their ports, drag a header, and move over the rows, chips, sliders and links. This needs a mouse or trackpad.

1 · TRACE THE SIGNAL WHOLE CHAIN

Hover an operator and the signal runs downstream, one hop after another. Each cable brightens as the pulse reaches it and each operator it feeds picks up a border. Hover an out-port for just that cable, or an in-port to see what feeds it.

2 · THE VERB, TYPED

The label types itself out when it changes, the way the path does in the nav. It takes about 20ms a letter and never more than 200ms. It moves with the pointer while it types. With reduced motion it appears whole.

3 · VERB LOOK

INVERT is a true invert of whatever is behind each letter, so it reads on dark, on the paper view and across an edge. Over green it turns magenta.

VALUE ONLY flips between light and dark text by what the pointer is over. No colour shift, but a label that straddles an edge is one colour on both sides. CHIP is the first version.
