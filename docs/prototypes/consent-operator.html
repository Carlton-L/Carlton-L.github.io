Consent operator

Asks once whether a visit may be recorded. Every visit is counted without cookies either way; the answer only decides session replay. The REC chip in the nav shows the answer on every page and is how a visitor changes it.

Spec

  • Changed in the build, 2026-10-23: the drawings above show the accepted design. This list is what was built: no cable, the PATCH position under the chip, the entrance, and the chip sizes changed after review.
  • Operator, first time: sys · recorder, a fixed SYS operator. In PATCH it hangs under the REC chip, top right, its right edge lined up with the chip's. In READ it sits at the bottom of the reading column; on phones it spans the width. It floats over the page and never pushes it, so no card moves. No cable.
  • Entrance: half a second after the first page of a visit, it fades in over 220 ms, moving 16 px: down from the top in PATCH, up from the bottom in READ. It stays up across page changes without coming in again.
  • Operator, reopened from the chip: under the REC chip, top right, in PATCH and READ. On phones it spans the width under the nav. It drops in from the chip (200 ms, 8 px, fading in).
  • Header: the normal operator header. The status reads waiting, then ● rec or ○ off after an answer.
  • Body: the accent colour (it follows the visitor's palette) with near-black text.
  • Copy: "Mind if I record your visit? It helps me make the site better. Anything you type stays hidden."
  • Buttons: ▶ ok and ■ no thanks. Solid: the page's black behind green text and a green border, so they look like the site's outline buttons do on black. Hover tints them green.
  • Link: privacy → goes to /privacy/, which says that a "no thanks" visit is still counted without cookies.
  • After an answer: the operator leaves at once: it drops 16 px and fades out in 220 ms. The nav chip changes in the same instant, which is the confirmation. It doesn't come back on its own.
  • Closing without answering again: when reopened, clicking the chip again or pressing Escape closes it the same way and keeps the current answer.
  • Exit, decided 2026-10-22: drop and fade. Considered: slide down, slide left, shrink into the chip, wipe down. All but the wipe are compositor-only; drop and fade moves the least and reads as the quietest.
  • Performance: only translate and opacity animate, so there's no layout and no repaint per frame. will-change is set only while it moves. When the exit ends (animationend, with a timeout as a fallback) the element is hidden with display: none; it stays in the DOM because it persists across page changes. Placing the reopened operator needs one measurement of the chip.
  • REC chip: beside PATCH / READ on every page. Below 1280 px it is the size of the OPS chip and drops OFF; at 500 px and under it shows only its glyph. Its states are named with aria-label.
    • No answer: REC ?, breathing. Clicking it flashes the operator.
    • Yes: ● REC. No: ○ REC OFF.
    • After an answer, clicking it reopens the operator under the chip with the current answer ticked. Clicking it again closes it.
  • Reduced motion: the operator appears and disappears without moving, and the chip doesn't breathe.
  • Keyboard: opening from the chip moves focus to the current answer's button; closing returns focus to the chip.
