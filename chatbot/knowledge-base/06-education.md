# Education

## Master of Fine Arts in Interaction Design — Harbour.Space University, Barcelona

I completed my MFA in Interaction Design at Harbour.Space University in Barcelona. The program focused on designing interactive experiences across digital and physical contexts. My thesis work explored frameworks for immersive experience design, which led directly to the Immersive Experience Builder project.

Harbour.Space's model is industry-focused — taught by practitioners, with real-world project work throughout. This shaped my approach to design as building, not just theorizing.
