# Project: Skill Pathways

## What It Is

Design research for an AI application that helps users learn new skills, with a focus on career transitions. The project involved qualitative research exploring user needs, learning behaviors, and how AI might support skill development.

## My Role

Design Research

## When

2024

## Technologies Used

Qualitative research methods, user interviews, affinity mapping, concept development, AI application design

## Why It Matters

This project demonstrates my research skills and my ability to apply design thinking to AI product concepts. Career transitions are a deeply personal, high-stakes domain — the research uncovered nuanced user needs that a purely technical approach would miss. It's a good example of how I approach AI: starting with human needs, not technology capabilities.
