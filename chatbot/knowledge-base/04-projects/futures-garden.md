# Project: Futures Garden

## What It Is

An EU initiative exploring life in 2040 through conversations with "digital souls" — LLM-powered archetypes that visitors interact with through a physical Orb device. The experience uses NFC-enabled phygital objects to trigger different conversational personas, blending physical interaction with AI-generated dialogue.

## My Role

Experience Design & Prototyping

## When

2024

## Why It Matters

Futures Garden demonstrates how AI (specifically LLMs) can be embedded in physical experiences to create meaningful, explorative interactions. It's not a chatbot on a screen — it's a tangible, spatial experience where the physical objects you hold shape the AI conversation you have. This is the kind of work I'm most excited about: bridging digital intelligence with physical interaction.

## Technologies Used

LLM integration, NFC technology, physical prototyping, experience design, conversational AI
