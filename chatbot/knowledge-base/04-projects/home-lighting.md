# Project: Interactive Home Lighting

## What It Is

A computer vision presence detection system that dynamically controls home lighting based on occupancy and movement. Lights respond to where people are in a space — turning on, dimming, and adjusting based on real-time presence data.

## My Role

Systems Design — full design and implementation

## When

2024

## Technologies Used

Computer vision, presence detection algorithms, smart lighting APIs, sensor integration, systems architecture

## Why It Matters

This is a practical application of systems thinking — connecting computer vision, lighting hardware, and human behavior into a seamless experience. It's the kind of project that demonstrates how I think about technology: as interconnected systems, not isolated features.
