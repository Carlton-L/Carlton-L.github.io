# Project: FAST (Futures-as-a-Service)

## What It Is

FAST is a futures-as-a-service platform that leverages AI and network graph science for strategic foresight and scenario exploration. It helps organizations explore possible futures, identify emerging trends, and make better strategic decisions.

## My Role

Frontend Development & Product Design. I lead the product development at Futurity Systems, working across the full stack with a focus on the React/TypeScript frontend and AI integration layer.

## When

2025 — Present

## Technologies Used

React, TypeScript, AI/LLM APIs, network graph visualization, data visualization

## Why It Matters

FAST demonstrates how AI can be applied to complex, real-world strategic problems — not just chatbots or content generation, but structured analytical thinking. It's a production tool used by organizations for actual decision-making.
