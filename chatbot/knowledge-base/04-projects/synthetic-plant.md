# Project: Synthetic Plant Exploration

## What It Is

A project revisiting BEAM robotics with modern components — creating simple, analog-driven robotic plants as an exploration of emergent behavior. BEAM (Biology, Electronics, Aesthetics, Mechanics) robotics uses simple analog circuits instead of microcontrollers to create lifelike behaviors.

## My Role

Robotics & Exploration — concept, design, and fabrication

## When

2024

## Technologies Used

Analog electronics, BEAM robotics principles, solar cells, simple motor circuits, physical prototyping

## Why It Matters

Not every project needs to be complex. The Synthetic Plant explores how simple analog circuits can produce surprisingly organic, lifelike behaviors — movement that emerges from the interaction between light, circuits, and motors rather than being explicitly programmed. It's a reminder that elegant solutions don't always require sophisticated technology.
