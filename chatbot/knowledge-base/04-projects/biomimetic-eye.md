# Project: Biomimetic Eye Prototype

## What It Is

An animatronic eye mechanism exploring expressive robotic movement through custom mechanics, RGB illumination, and modular assembly. It's a robotics project focused on how machines can communicate emotion and intention through lifelike movement.

## My Role

Robotics & Prototyping — full design and fabrication

## When

2024

## Technologies Used

Servo motors, custom mechanical design, RGB LED systems, SolidWorks CAD, 3D printing, Arduino/microcontroller programming

## Why It Matters

This project showcases my hardware engineering roots applied to expressive, human-centered robotics. It's a physical prototype, not just a simulation — something you can hold and watch move. It demonstrates mechanical design, electronics integration, and an eye for biomimetic aesthetics.
