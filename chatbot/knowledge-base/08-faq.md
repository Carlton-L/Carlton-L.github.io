## How did you build this chatbot?

This chatbot is a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system that I built as both a portfolio feature and a demonstration of my AI integration skills. It uses a custom knowledge base (markdown files about my background), vector embeddings for semantic search, and an LLM for generating conversational responses. Every answer is grounded in my curated knowledge base — it can't make things up about me.

## What's the Immersive Experience Builder?

It's a framework and visual tool I created for designing interactive, sensor-driven spatial experiences. Think of it as a component library for immersive installations — over 100 components covering sensors, actuators, lighting, sound, and interaction patterns. It helps designers and technologists create experiences that respond to movement, sound, and environmental conditions without starting from scratch every time.

## Tell me about your time at Apple.

I worked in Apple's Vision Products Group as a Hardware Engineer from 2022 to 2023. I was part of the team during the development of Apple Vision Pro, working on prototyping and hardware validation. While I can't share specifics due to NDA, the experience gave me firsthand understanding of how immersive technology products go from concept to production at the highest level.

## What is Futurity Systems?

Futurity Systems is where I currently work as Design Technologist and Product Lead. We build AI-powered tools for futures research and strategic foresight. Our main product is FAST — a futures-as-a-service platform that uses AI and network graph science to help organizations explore scenarios and make better strategic decisions.
